Account Deletion Request

How to request deletion of your UCI Sarthi account, what data is deleted, and what is retained by law.

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data held by Upendra Competition Institute ("UCI Sarthi"). This page explains the process, what gets deleted, and what must be retained under Indian law.

How to request deletion

Account deletion is processed manually by our team. Send a deletion request by email to our grievance officer. Include your registered email address — the one you used to sign in with Google.

Email: hello@ucisarthi.in

  • We will acknowledge your request within 48 hours and complete deletion within 30 days.
  • Deletion is not automatic. We verify your identity before processing the request to protect your account from unauthorised deletion.

What we delete

  • Your name, email address and profile photo (from Google Sign-In).
  • Phone number, city and target exam (added during onboarding).
  • All test attempts, answers, scores and performance history.
  • Lesson progress and bookmarked notes.
  • Push notification subscriptions (web browser and Android app).
  • Any enquiries or messages you submitted via the contact form.
  • Your account login access — you will no longer be able to sign in.
  • Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

What we retain and why

  • Payment records and order IDs — retained for 7 years to comply with Indian GST, accounting and tax law. This data is not used for any other purpose.
  • Anonymised, aggregated test-attempt statistics (no personal identifiers) — retained indefinitely for academic analytics.
  • Retained data is not shared with third parties and is used only as required by law.

Effect on your account

  • After deletion is complete you will no longer be able to sign in to UCI Sarthi with your Google account.
  • Any paid course or test-pass access will be permanently revoked. Refund eligibility is not affected by a deletion request and follows our Refund Policy.
  • If you change your mind, you may create a new account in the future, but your previous data will not be restored.
